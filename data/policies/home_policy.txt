HOME INSURANCE POLICY INFORMATION

Section: Fire Damage Coverage
Home insurance policies generally cover structural damage caused by accidental fire incidents.

Coverage may include:
- structural repair expenses
- temporary accommodation costs
- replacement of damaged household belongings

Coverage is subject to policy limits and verification procedures.

================================================================================

Section: Theft Coverage
Theft-related claims may require:
- police complaint documentation
- ownership proof
- damage assessment reports

Claim settlement depends on:
- policy terms
- verification procedures
- deductible application

================================================================================

Section: Water Damage Coverage
Water damage caused by burst pipes or accidental leakage may be covered under certain policy plans.

Coverage exclusions may apply for:
- poor maintenance
- long-term leakage
- intentional negligence

================================================================================

Section: Natural Disaster Coverage
Some home insurance policies include protection against:
- storms
- earthquakes
- floods
- lightning damage

Coverage availability depends on:
- policy type
- selected coverage add-ons
- regional eligibility

================================================================================

Section: Temporary Accommodation
If the insured home becomes temporarily uninhabitable due to a covered event, the insurer may provide reimbursement for temporary accommodation expenses within policy limits.

================================================================================

Section: General Exclusions
Common exclusions include:
- intentional damage
- illegal activities
- fraudulent claims
- normal wear and tear
- damages occurring outside policy validity
