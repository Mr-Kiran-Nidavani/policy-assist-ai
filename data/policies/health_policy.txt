HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY INFORMATION

Section: Cataract Surgery Coverage
Cataract surgery is covered under the health insurance policy when deemed medically necessary by a licensed ophthalmologist. Coverage becomes eligible after completion of the 24-month waiting period from the policy effective date.

Coverage may include:
- hospitalization expenses
- surgeon consultation fees
- operation theater charges
- prescribed medications during hospitalization

Pre-authorization may be required for planned procedures performed at network hospitals.

Cosmetic or elective eye procedures are not covered.

================================================================================

Section: Maternity Coverage
Maternity benefits become eligible after a continuous waiting period of 36 months from the policy start date.

Coverage may include:
- hospitalization expenses during childbirth
- medically necessary prenatal care
- postnatal treatment expenses within policy limits

Fertility treatments and elective procedures are excluded.

================================================================================

Section: Deductibles
A deductible is the amount paid by the policyholder before insurance coverage becomes applicable.

Example:
If the deductible is $500 and the approved claim amount is $5,000, the first $500 must be paid by the customer.

Deductible amounts vary depending on:
- coverage category
- policy type
- treatment type

================================================================================

Section: Claim Reimbursement Process
For reimbursement claims, the following documents are generally required:
- completed claim form
- original hospital invoices
- discharge summary
- prescriptions
- diagnostic reports
- proof of payment

Incomplete documentation may delay claim processing.

Claim approval depends on:
- policy terms
- exclusions
- waiting periods
- claim verification procedures

Claim reimbursement amounts are not guaranteed.

================================================================================

Section: General Exclusions
The following are commonly excluded:
- cosmetic surgery without medical necessity
- self-inflicted injuries
- fraudulent claims
- illegal activities
- treatments outside policy validity
