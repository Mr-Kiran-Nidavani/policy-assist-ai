# Insurance Policy Update Process

## Overview
This document outlines the procedures for updating insurance policies through various channels including chatbot, self-service portal, and licensed agents.

## Updatable Information

### Contact Details (Self-Service)
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Updated via: Chatbot, Self-Service Portal, or Licensed Agent

### Personal Information
- Driver information
- Vehicle details
- Coverage preferences
- Updated via: Licensed Agent, Self-Service Portal

### Coverage Changes
- Deductible amounts
- Additional endorsements
- Coverage limits
- **Note:** Premium may increase or decrease based on changes

## Update Process

### Step 1: Provide Required Details
- Submit current policy information
- Provide new details requiring updates
- Verify identity through security questions

### Step 2: Document Verification
- Submit supporting documents if required
- Examples: Driver's license, vehicle registration, proof of address

### Step 3: Review and Approval
- Simple updates: Automated approval via self-service
- Complex changes: Licensed agent review required
- Policy updated upon approval

### Step 4: Confirmation
- Receive update confirmation via email/SMS
- New policy documents issued if applicable

## Not Allowed - Restrictions

### Effective Date Changes
- ❌ Cannot backdate policy effective dates
- ❌ Cannot make changes retroactive to past billing periods

### Policy Cancellation
- ❌ Cannot cancel via chatbot for pending claims
- ❌ Cancellation requires licensed agent review
- ⚠️ Subject to cancellation policy terms and conditions

### Premium Modifications
- ❌ Cannot manually adjust premiums outside system
- Premiums recalculated based on approved updates only
