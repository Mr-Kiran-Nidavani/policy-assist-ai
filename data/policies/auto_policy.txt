AUTO INSURANCE POLICY INFORMATION

Section: Collision Coverage
Collision coverage helps pay for repair expenses resulting from accidents involving another vehicle or object.

Coverage is subject to:
- deductibles
- approved claim assessment
- policy limits

Damage caused by intentional misuse or unauthorized driving is excluded.

================================================================================

Section: Comprehensive Coverage
Comprehensive auto insurance may cover:
- theft
- fire damage
- natural disasters
- vandalism

Coverage depends on:
- policy limits
- insurer verification
- claim assessment

================================================================================

Section: Adding a Vehicle
Policyholders may request the addition of a new vehicle to an existing policy.

Required information may include:
- vehicle registration details
- purchase invoice
- vehicle identification number (VIN)

Coverage activation is subject to underwriting review and policy approval.

================================================================================

Section: Adding a Driver
Additional drivers may be added after:
- identity verification
- driving license validation
- insurer approval

The insurer may review:
- driving history
- accident history
- driver age

Premium adjustments may apply.

================================================================================

Section: Accident Claims
For accident-related claims, customers may be required to submit:
- accident photographs
- police reports
- repair estimates
- vehicle inspection reports

Claim settlement depends on:
- policy coverage
- investigation findings
- deductible application

================================================================================

Section: General Exclusions
The following situations are commonly excluded:
- driving under the influence
- unauthorized drivers
- intentional damage
- illegal vehicle usage
- expired policy coverage
