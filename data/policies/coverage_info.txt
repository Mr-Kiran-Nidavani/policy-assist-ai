Section: Collision Coverage

Collision coverage helps pay for repair expenses resulting from accidents involving another vehicle or object.

Coverage Conditions:
- deductible applies before claim payout
- reimbursement is limited to policy limits
- claim requires approved damage assessment

Covered Incidents:
- vehicle collision accidents
- impact with poles or barriers
- rollover accidents

Deductible:
- standard deductible: ₹2,000
- voluntary deductible options available

Common Exclusions:
- intentional damage
- unauthorized driving
- illegal racing activities

Section: Comprehensive Coverage

Comprehensive coverage helps pay for non-collision vehicle damage.

Coverage Conditions:
- claim subject to policy coverage limits
- deductible applies for each approved claim

Covered Incidents:
- theft
- fire damage
- flood and cyclone damage
- vandalism
- falling objects

Deductible:
- standard deductible: ₹1,500
- natural disaster deductible may apply

Common Exclusions:
- wear and tear
- mechanical failure
- uninsured modifications

Section: Maternity Coverage

Maternity coverage helps pay for medical expenses related to pregnancy, childbirth, and newborn care.

Coverage Conditions:
- waiting period must be completed before maternity benefits become active
- coverage is subject to policy sub-limits
- treatment must be taken at approved or registered medical facilities

Covered Expenses:
- normal delivery expenses
- cesarean section expenses
- pre-natal consultations and diagnostic tests
- post-natal medical care
- newborn baby coverage for specified duration

Waiting Period:
- standard waiting period: 24 months
- some policies may have waiting periods up to 36 months
- maternity claims during waiting period are not covered

Deductible:
- standard deductible: ₹5,000 per claim
- co-payment may apply depending on policy plan

Coverage Limits:
- normal delivery coverage up to ₹50,000
- cesarean delivery coverage up to ₹75,000
- newborn coverage available for first 90 days

Common Exclusions:
- maternity expenses during waiting period
- fertility or infertility treatments
- elective pregnancy termination unless medically necessary
- non-medical hospital consumables

Claim Notes:
- hospitalization records and discharge summary required
- claim approval subject to policy verification
- expenses exceeding policy sub-limits are payable by customer
