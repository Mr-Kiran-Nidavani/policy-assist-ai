# General Claim Process Documentation

## Overview
This document outlines the standard procedures, required documentation, and limitations for processing claims.

---

## Claim Process Steps

### 1. Claim Initiation
- Submit claim within specified timeframe
- Provide claimant information and contact details
- Assign unique claim reference number

### 2. Documentation Review
- Verify all required documents are provided
- Check document authenticity and completeness
- Request additional information if needed

### 3. Assessment
- Evaluate claim eligibility
- Review policy coverage and terms
- Conduct investigation if necessary

### 4. Decision
- Approve, deny, or request amendments
- Provide written decision with reasoning
- Communicate outcome to claimant

### 5. Resolution
- Process payment or denial notification
- Update claim status
- Archive documentation

---

## Required Documents

- Original claim form (completed and signed)
- Valid identification/proof of eligibility
- Supporting evidence (receipts, invoices, medical records, etc.)
- Policy documentation
- Proof of loss or damage
- Bank details for payment (if approved)

---

## Limitations

- Claims must be filed within 30 days of incident
- Maximum coverage limits per policy
- Exclusions apply per policy terms
- Fraudulent claims will be rejected and reported
- Processing time: 10-15 business days
- No retroactive claims beyond policy start date

---

## Contact & Support

For inquiries, contact Claims Department with reference number.
